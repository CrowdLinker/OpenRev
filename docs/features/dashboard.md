# Using the dashboard

## Overview

The top row shows **Visitors, Revenue, Conversion rate** (visitors who paid ÷ visitors), **Revenue per visitor, Bounce rate, Session time** and **Online now**, each compared with the previous period of the same length. Click a metric to plot it; click **Revenue** to toggle the revenue bars.

Below the chart, every panel shows visitors **and** revenue:

| Panel | Tabs |
|---|---|
| Sources | Channel, Referrer, Campaign, UTM source, Medium, Ref |
| Pages | Page, Entry, Exit, Exit links (outbound clicks), Hostname |
| Locations | Map, Country, Region, City |
| Devices | Device, Browser, OS |
| Goals | Goals with visitors, completions, conversion rate and revenue — click one for its custom properties |
| Funnel | Your funnels, with drop-off between steps |

Hover a row for bounce rate, session time, customers, conversion rate and revenue per visitor. Click a row to **filter the whole dashboard** (e.g. Country is France → see which sources make money in France). **Details** opens the full sortable table.

## Date ranges

Presets from *Today* to *All time*, or a custom range. Hover a preset and click the 📌 pin to keep it as a quick button (stored per website in your browser). Change the chart granularity with Hourly / Daily / Weekly / Monthly. The date range and filters live in the URL, so any view can be bookmarked or shared with teammates.

## Notes

Use **Note** to annotate the chart ("Launched on Product Hunt", "Started Google Ads"). Notes appear as markers on the chart for everyone on the team.

## Realtime

A live globe of current visitors, a list of active visitors with their current page, source, location, device and a **buyer-intent score** (based on how often their channel converts, whether they're returning, pages like pricing/checkout, goals and identification), plus an activity feed of pageviews, goals and payments.

## Visitors & journeys

Search visitors by name, email, user id or visitor id, show only customers, or visitors who completed a goal. A visitor profile shows lifetime revenue, days to convert, the first touch, custom properties and the full **journey**: every session with its source, every page, goal, outbound click and payment.

## Revenue

Net revenue, new revenue, renewals, refunds, payments, average order value and customers; revenue by channel, referrer, campaign, country and landing page; **customer lifetime value** by first touch; and every payment with its attributed source.

## Funnels

Build funnels from page visits (equals / starts with / contains), goals and payments, with a live preview. Each step shows visitors, conversion from the first step, drop-off and revenue.
